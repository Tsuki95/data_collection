# [ Derivatives of Bis(diphenylphosphino)difluoromethane: the Disulfide Ph2P(=
S)CF2P(= S)Ph2 and the Bis[chlorogold(I)] Complex
Ph2P(AuCl)CF2P(AuCl)Ph2](https://journals.iucr.org/c/issues/1996/10/00/fg1184/fg1184.pdf)

[P. G. Jones](https://scripts.iucr.org/cgi-
bin/citedin?search_on=name&author_name=Jones, P.G.) and [E.
Bembenek](https://scripts.iucr.org/cgi-
bin/citedin?search_on=name&author_name=Bembenek, E.)

The title compound difluoromethylenebis(diphenylphosphine) disulfide,
C25H20F2P2S2, displays an S1—P1P2—S2 torsion angle of −90.76 (5)°. The P—CF2
bond lengths of 1.893 (2) and 1.893 (3) Å are appreciably lengthened in
comparison to systems involving the non-fluorinated analogue
diphenylphosphinomethane. In the complex _μ_
-[difluoromethylenebis(diphenylphosphine)-_P,P_ ']-bis[chlorogold(I)],
[Au2Cl2(C25H20F2P2)], the Au—PP—Au torsion angle is 31.2 (1)° and a short
intramolecular AuAu contact of 3.1432 (8) Å is observed.
