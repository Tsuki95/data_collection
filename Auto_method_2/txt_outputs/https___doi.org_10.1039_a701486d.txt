##  Synthesis of dinuclear gold-(I) and -(II) complexes by reaction of
[AuI2{µ-(CH2)2 PPh2}2] with protonic acids

[Manuel Bardají](/en/results?searchtext=Author%3AManuel%20Bardaj%C3%AD),__
[Elena Cerrada](/en/results?searchtext=Author%3AElena%20Cerrada),__   [Peter
G. Jones](/en/results?searchtext=Author%3APeter%20G.%20Jones),__   [Antonio
Laguna](/en/results?searchtext=Author%3AAntonio%20Laguna) __  and  [Mariano
Laguna](/en/results?searchtext=Author%3AMariano%20Laguna) __  

### Abstract

The reaction of the bis(ylide) gold complex [Au 2 {µ-(CH 2 ) 2 PPh 2 } 2 ]
with strong acids (such as HClO 4 ) in the presence of diphosphines L–L
afforded a mixture of heterobridged gold(I) complexes [Au 2 {µ-(CH 2 ) 2 PPh 2
}(µ-L–L)] + (L–L = Ph 2 PCH 2 CH 2 PPh 2 or Ph 2 PCH 2 PPh 2 ) and [PPh 2 Me 2
] + . After addition of Li(tcnq), [Au 2 {µ-(CH 2 ) 2 PPh 2 }(µ-L–L)] 2 [ClO 4
][tcnq] were obtained pure (tcnq = 7,7,8,8-tetracyanoquinodimethane). The
crystal structure of the Ph 2 PCH 2 CH 2 PPh 2 derivative shows an
intramolecular gold–gold distance of 3.1294(9) Å. The use of weak protonic
acids such as pyridine-2-thiol (C 5 H 5 NS) or 2-sulfanylbenzothiazole (C 7 H
5 NS 2 ), in the presence of atmospheric oxygen, led to complexes of gold(II).
The crystal structure of [Au II 2 {µ-(CH 2 ) 2 PPh  2 } 2 (C 5 H 4 NS) 2  ]
shows a gold–gold bond length of 2.6686(13) Å.
