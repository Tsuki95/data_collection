## Abstract

SC6H5 was substituted for Cl in AuCl(PPh3) and (AuCl)2(trans-Ph2PCH=CHPPh2) by
reacting with Sn(SC6H5)(n-Bu)3 in refluxing THF. Both products exhibit new
three-dimensional motifs which are created by Au···Au interaction and π-π
interaction between phenyl rings in crystals.
