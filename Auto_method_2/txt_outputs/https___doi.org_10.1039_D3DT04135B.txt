### Abstract

The marine natural product norzooanemonin
(1,3-dimethylimidazolium-4-carboxylate) has been used to prepare a series of
carboxyl- or carboxylate-functionalized N-heterocyclic carbene (NHC) gold(I)
complexes from [(Me2S)AuCl] in the presence of potassium carbonate. The
potential of the resulting mono- and dicarbene complexes to act as cytotoxic
or antibacterial drugs was investigated.
