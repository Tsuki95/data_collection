isonitrile neutral complexes

[Oussama Elbjeirami](/en/results?searchtext=Author%3AOussama%20Elbjeirami),_a_

### Abstract

A new compound that exhibits the shortest intermolecular Au⋯Au distance ever
reported for neutral RNCAuX complexes is found to exhibit a counterintuitive
higher-energy Au-centered phosphorescence than that in an analogous compound
with a much longer Au⋯Au distance, presumably due to a different extent of
excited-state distortion in dimers _vs_. extended chains.
