### Abstract

Gold–isocyanide complexes XAu(RNC) (X = halide, pseudohalide, R = alkyl, aryl)
and water soluble gold–carbene complexes XAuC(NHPh)[MeN(CH2CH2O)_n_ Me] (X =
Cl, _n_ = 1–11) have been prepared and evaluated as substrates for the direct
laser writing of gold decoration onto ceramics.
