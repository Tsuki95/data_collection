### Abstract

Bimetallic complexes [(η5-C5R5)M(S2N2)Au(PPh3)][ClO4] and tetrametallic
species [{(η5-C5R5)M(S2N2)Au}2(μ2-P^P)][ClO4]2 (R = H, M = Co; R = Me, M = Ir;
P^P = dppm or dppe) can be prepared by treatment of [(η5-C5R5)M(S2N2)] with
gold(I) electrophiles generated by chloride abstraction from [AuCl(PPh3)] or
[(AuCl)2(μ2-P^P)]. X-Ray crystallography of [(η5-C5Me5)Ir(S2N2)Au(PPh3)][ClO4]
and [{(η5-C5Me5)Ir(S2N2)Au}2(μ2-dppm)][ClO4]2 confirms auration of the metal-
bound nitrogen atom of the MS2N2 ring. π-Stacking of theMS2N2 rings occurs
within both structures.
